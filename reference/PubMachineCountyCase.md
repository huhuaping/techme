# Typical Cases of Full Mechanization for Specialty Economic Crops

A data set containing typical cases of full-process mechanization for
specialty economic crops (vegetables, fruit trees, tea, and medicinal
herbs), compiled year by year from public notices of the Ministry of
Agriculture and Rural Affairs <https://www.moa.gov.cn>, with wide data
format.

## Usage

``` r
PubMachineCountyCase
```

## Format

A data frame:

- year:

  integer, the data year aligned with the source file

- batch:

  integer, the official batch number

- category:

  character, the crop group (vegetables, fruit trees, tea, or medicinal
  herbs)

- index:

  integer, the sequence number within the year and batch

- order:

  integer, the sequence number within the crop group

- title:

  character, the original case title in the public notice

- province:

  character, province names in reduced chinese

- place:

  character, city, county, or region parsed from the title

- crop:

  character, crop or variety parsed from the title

## Source

<https://www.huhuaping.com/>
